A path through disciplines

Different fields, one way of thinking.

I have moved through engineering, mathematics, classrooms, and technology. What connects them is a fascination with structure, people, and the work of turning an idea into something useful.

Ground

Engineering taught me to think in consequences.

Infrastructure and energy made complexity tangible. Decisions had weight, constraints were real, and every model eventually had to meet people, materials, budgets, and time.

Language

Mathematics gave that instinct a deeper language.

Studying and teaching mathematics sharpened how I see patterns and explain difficult ideas. Education also made one thing clear: understanding is designed together, never simply delivered.

Now

Products and data science bring the threads together.

Today I build at the intersection of product, data, AI, and education. I care about systems that are rigorous underneath, clear on the surface, and genuinely useful to the people using them.
