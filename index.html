Hello — I’m Tuto.

I’m Carlos López González. Friends and colleagues often call me Tuto. This site — tutofractal.com — is my personal space: where engineering, mathematics, teaching, and visual curiosity meet.

I started as a civil engineer in Buenos Aires, working on infrastructure and waste-to-energy projects where timelines, budgets, and models had to hold up in the real world. That pull toward quantitative structure led me into mathematics — an MA at SFSU, teaching, research on math–art cognition — and into building software and ML systems you can actually use.

Today I care about the same through-line: civil engineer → quantitative modeling → mathematics → deployed systems. Sometimes that looks like Atlas for instructors; sometimes fractals and TDA; sometimes probability tooling or on-device vision.
