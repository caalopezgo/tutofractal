Music 25 · 2020

Poems for music.

A collection written for rhythm, dance, memory, and the Caribbean.

01

Church of Engineering

La Sevillana came into the church
to ask if she could pray
wearing hippie shorts
but she was asked to leave
and please to sin no more.

She was gifted a sad guitar
La Sevillana took her tragic love
and her new sad guitar
to where the orchids turn silver.
There, she played the guitar
to heal together
but the guitar wasn’t able
to love anymore.

02

Aché

Hey Yoruba,
you that know
how to do some magic
with six tones
How to call Yemayá
with red flowers,
candles,
and a rough Batá

You that met all the Orishas
look at me,
I am already dressing
a white shirt
would you give me
some good luck?

03

Fiesta en el Patio

Las mujeres le cantaban
palabras escondidas
al viejo patio del arrabal
Y el palo de mango se mecía
sobre los techos de zinc
que no entendían
la rumba ni el carnaval

Y todos tarareaban lindo
la salsa negra,
el caribe indio
Todos bailaban
la danza del manglar
esa que vacuna sin preguntar

04

Odysseus

When I came to the city
I lost my Plena, I lost my dreams
I heard a Cuatro singing to me
Then, I became a kid
and when the city came to me
it brought me drums
that burnt my arms
until my hands
became hammers
that bombed my blood
right back home

05

Sueste

Como los aguaceros de mil colores
que mezclan arena y mar
así se nos vino encima
la calle calma al despertar

La lluvia nos arrebató el sueño
que nos vigilaba
y el sudor de una noche en vela
nos regalaba pena

Le subiste el volumen a la radio
para escuchar a los muertos cantores
que después de morir, reviven
como los aguaceros de mil colores

06

Everybody is Looking for Mambo

If you are coming to San Francisco
be sure you get mambo in your hair
be sure to stay up on the coast
until you fall in love
with the bay devils
and some beautiful drunks

Unless it’s later than 3 a.m.
don’t call the devils
rather stay by yourself
but if you are still dress
don’t even think to go to their place
and if by chance you sell peanuts
put them inside their maracas
and leave them quiet apart
before the mambo start

07

Pimienta en Lata

Cuando descifres
lo que para mí es misterio
mi truco se transformará
en cuerdas y
siete golpes de tambor

Entonces para Cuba me iré
a buscar la melodía
del son montuno
que no está muerta
sino perdida

Y cuando todo tenga sentido
va a venir una mulata
a mezclar miel
pimienta y sal

08

Café del Campo

En la isla
donde los pájaros sin alas
vuelan en burbujas de café,
una guagua me dijo al pasar
que amar es como gozar
pero más barato

09

Lord Calypso

If you want to know
what time is it
and what’s going around your town
If you want to know
what the river brought last night
and how hot was the fire
that made the rocks

Even when you want to know
where the pain is coming from,
ask a Calypsonian,
they have all the answers

10

Llora que Llora

Sandra Mora, Jota provinciana
ponete a bailar como te de la gana,
mezclá lo hablado con letras vascas
para que se te olvide tu pasado

11

Durmiente y Dulzón / Mar Salado

Mi corazón danzón
nació en Matanzas
también el tuyo
y con él, tu orgullo

En Matanzas, los bellos
no son durmientes
ni trovan en clave
al cruzar los puentes

El aire,
bandolero
como el dulce,
se va

12

The Time Manifesto

A spectre is haunting the wall
is the sprectre of the revolution
that moves in clockwise and
don’t want us to know the time

Even when we said it goodbye
for welcoming the Jazz
the spectre laughed at us
and since then it’s scared us

13

No Nos Lamentemos

Loco, salga contento
bien cargadito
de pensamientos
y felicidad

Cante, cante
por toda la ciudad
pues usted es indio de aquí
y también del más allá

14

Las Estrellas de la Mafia

Heroes also remove their clothes
before going to sleep at night
They are miserable sometimes
so don’t pretend to be surprised
when you see them
kissing the godfather ring
and walking down the streets
with a little mafia-style

Dear heroes,
please wear some gold jewelry
and your aviator sunglasses
Bring Maelo to the party
with his caribean clothes
because he has the clave
every time he snaps his thumbs

15

Anclar en el Medio del Mar

El santero de pelo largo
me cuenta que sus muertos
tocan instrumentos
y yo le digo,
esos son puros inventos
de santero de pelo largo

Pero él insiste
en soñar con muertos
y con santos amigos
que son clandestinos
porque a los vivos
los colecciona uno a uno
en el cajón de los enemigos

16

You Better Hang on to a Sloppy God

My crude,
there is a land of life
and water and wood,
my crude,
that you will never see
because it doesn’t exist
except you become a panther.

That is the land of the black gods
who live together with stray dogs.
They don’t have any piano
but if you want you can still dance
with a walking bass
