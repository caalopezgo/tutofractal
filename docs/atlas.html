Project brief

Atlas

An academic intelligence layer for instructors and academic units. AI suggests; humans decide.

Open live pilot Back to projects
Concept

What it is

Atlas helps instructors prepare class, grade with rubrics, and spot at-risk students — and gives departments a shared layer for coordination. It is not “only a Canvas connector.” Professors can start by uploading course materials; LMS connect is an upgrade path.

Doors in

Two entry paths

A — Bring your course: upload syllabus, PDFs, rubrics, and assignments; get prep and feedback help without IT approval.

B — Connect LMS: live courses, rosters, submissions, and later write-back of approved outputs.

Pilot

What is live now

Instructor pilot with private workspaces, course materials, Prepare drafts, and Assessment Intelligence (grade drafts with evidence and human review). Sample multi-role demo remains available for academic-unit walkthroughs.
