Project brief

Atlas

Instructors and academic units need help preparing class, giving feedback, and noticing struggling students — without an autopilot that changes grades or contacts students. Atlas is that intelligence layer: AI suggests; humans decide.

Open live pilot Contact via LinkedIn
Beachhead

Start without LMS approval

Instructors bring syllabus, PDFs, rubrics, and assignments. They get prep drafts and assessment feedback workflows immediately. Connecting Canvas (or another LMS) is an upgrade for live rosters, submissions, and later write-back — not a gate.

Live now

Instructor pilot capabilities

Private workspaces (Supabase Auth + RLS), course materials upload/preview, Prepare (lesson drafts grounded in materials), and Assessment Intelligence (score/feedback drafts with evidence and human review states). A separate sample multi-role demo remains for department walkthroughs.
