Visual

Math, image, structure.

TutoFractal is not only a domain name. Fractals, recursive form, and diagrammatic thinking run through the work — from interactive Mandelbrot/Julia exploration to thesis research on how students reason across mathematics and art (Fibonacci spiral and Hokusai’s Great Wave), presented at JMM.

The site background is an original drip composition (inspired by abstract expressionism, not a reproduction of any Pollock painting). A curated photo/art gallery will land here when I publish personal images; until then, the research and fractal tooling carry the visual thread.
